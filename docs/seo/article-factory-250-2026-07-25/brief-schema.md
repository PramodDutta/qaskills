# Article Brief Schema

Every selected topic must have a complete brief before article writing starts.

## Required Fields

- `batch`: integer from 1 through 50
- `domain`: one of the eight approved mining domains
- `slug`: unique canonical blog slug
- `primaryKeyword`: three to seven words
- `title`: starts with the exact primary keyword and remains within 60 characters after the
  ` | QASkills.sh` suffix
- `description`: 140 to 155 characters and contains the exact primary keyword
- `intent`: informational, how-to, comparison, troubleshooting, or commercial
- `coreQuestion`: the question answered in the first 40 to 60 words
- `secondaryKeywords`: five to eight unique long-tail phrases
- `outline`: eight to twelve H2 sections, including at least three question headings,
  `Frequently Asked Questions`, and a final `Conclusion`
- `tablePlan`: the concrete comparison or data table and its source
- `procedurePlan`: a numbered procedure with at least three steps
- `codeExamples`: at least two examples tied to verified repository files
- `repoEvidence`: two to five repository paths that exist and will be cited in the article
- `internalRoutes`: at least five verified routes, including `/skills`
- `relatedSlugs`: four registered article slugs
- `sources`: two to four verified authoritative HTTPS URLs
- `cta`: a specific final action pointing to a verified QASkills route

## Writing Contract

The body must contain 3,000 to 4,000 code-excluded words. It must use the primary keyword in
the opening 100 words at one to three percent weighted density. Each secondary keyword must
appear in an H2 or H3. FAQ answers contain 40 to 60 words. Paragraphs contain two to four
sentences. Average sentence length is 15 to 20 words, and Flesch Reading Ease is 58 to 72.

All external links must appear in the brief source list. All repository evidence paths must
exist and appear in the article. The publication gate rejects placeholders, banned phrases,
em dashes, non-ASCII punctuation, unregistered routes, duplicate prose, or unapproved topics.
